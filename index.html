Campus First Aid & CPR Quick Guide

1. Hands-Only CPR for Sudden Cardiac Arrest

  1. Check responsiveness: Tap shoulders and shout "Are you okay?".
  2. Call 911 or hit Campus SOS immediately. Direct someone to fetch the nearest AED station.
  3. Push hard and fast in the center of the chest to the beat of "Stayin' Alive" (100-120 beats/min). Push at least 2 inches deep.

2. Using the Campus Automated Defibrillator (AED)

  • Turn on the AED unit. Visual and vocal prompts will guide you.
  • Apply sticky electrode pads to bare chest as shown on the diagrams.
  • AED automatically analyzes heart rhythm. Stand clear if a shock is advised.

3. Severe Bleeding Control

Apply firm, direct pressure with sterile gauze or clean cloth. Elevate the wounded limb if possible. Keep constant pressure until campus medical staff arrives.
